Our storyWhy "The One"?

To be the ONE with great products and service to serve our local area.


The One is a unique premium boutique serving the Lehigh Valley with major brands from across the world — the store in downtown Allentown where you can find premium outerwear without the trip to King of Prussia or North Jersey.

PUMA and Nike sell all year long. When the cold hits, it's all about Moose Knuckles, Timberland and Nobis.
